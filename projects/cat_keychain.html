Cat Keychain

A tiny cat-shaped keychain.

About the project

There's also the option to play Brick Breaker on it. If you have any ideas for things to add, feel free to message me on Instagram.

What I used

  • ATTINY3216-SNR
  • 100nF 0805 Ceramic Capacitors
  • 470Ω Resistor
  • 220Ω Resistor
  • 0603 red LED Indication
  • Battery Clip
  • CR2032
  • 2x 6x6mm Tactile Switches
  • SMD 4x3mm Tactile Switches
